1. Information Collection and Use

DexReader can be browsed without an account. Creating an account unlocks favorites, reading history, and reading statistics, which are synchronised across your devices. When you sign in, the following data is collected and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure:

Account information

  • Email address and authentication credentials, handled by Firebase Authentication. Your password is never stored or readable by me.
  • Account identifier assigned by Firebase Authentication.
  • Display name and, if you provide one, an avatar image URL. Both are values you enter yourself and can change at any time in the app.

App data

  • Favorites — the manga you have marked as favorite.
  • Reading history — which manga and chapter you opened, the last page you reached in each chapter, and when you last read it.
  • Reading statistics — the total time spent reading per calendar day, used to render the reading-activity charts in the app.

This data is used solely to provide and improve the features described above. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Some data never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to me or to any third party:

  • Chapter cache — recently opened chapter pages are cached locally so they load faster and can be re-read without re-downloading.
  • App preferences — your theme choice and whether you have completed the onboarding screens.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this local data.

3. Third-Party Services

The app relies on third-party services that may collect information used to identify you, in order to deliver content and support the application's infrastructure:

  • Firebase Authentication — account sign-up, sign-in, and password reset.
  • Cloud Firestore — storage of your profile, favorites, reading history, and reading statistics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring — app startup and network performance measurement.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — aggregate usage measurement.
  • MangaDex API — the source of all manga metadata, cover art, and chapter images. Requests to load content reach MangaDex servers directly from your device.

Privacy policies of these providers:

  • Google Privacy Policy (Google Play Services and Google Analytics)
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • MangaDex

4. Log and Diagnostic Data

When the app encounters an error, diagnostic data is collected through Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring. This Log Data may include your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device model and name, operating system version, app configuration and version, the time and date of use, and the stack trace of the failure. It is used solely for debugging, stability, and performance work.

5. Data Retention

Your account data, favorites, reading history, and reading statistics are retained for as long as your account exists. They are removed when your account is deleted, as described in the next section. Diagnostic data collected by Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring is retained according to Google's own retention schedules, which are described in the Firebase privacy documentation linked above.

6. Data Deletion Request

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data — your email address, display name, avatar URL, favorites, reading history, and reading statistics stored in Cloud Firestore.

How to request deletion. Send an email from the address registered to your DexReader account to the support address in the Contact section, with the subject line "Delete my DexReader account". Your request will be processed within 7 business days, after which the data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.

You can also change or clear your display name and avatar at any time from the Profile screen in the app, and you can remove individual favorites and individual reading-history entries from the Favorites and History screens.

7. Cookies

Cookies are small data files commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers, sent to your browser by the websites you visit and stored on your device. This Service does not use cookies explicitly. However, the third-party libraries the app depends on may use them to collect information and improve their services.

8. Security

I value your trust in providing your personal information, and I use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Account data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure and access is restricted by security rules so that each account can read and write only its own records. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, and no method of electronic storage, is 100% secure. I cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. External Content and Links

All manga metadata, cover art, and chapter images displayed in the app are fetched from the MangaDex API and are shown inside the app. DexReader does not host this content and is not responsible for it. This policy page itself contains links to the privacy policies of the third-party services listed above; those sites are not operated by me, and I advise you to review their own policies.
